Educational Navigation System / the long-term ambition

The Vision

A clearer picture of learning.
For everyone who helps it happen.

Empowered Teachers

Connect classroom observations to a richer understanding of each learner—with evidence they can question and judgment that remains theirs.

Involved Parents

Understand the story beyond a grade and take part in meaningful conversations about their child’s learning, with appropriate privacy and sharing boundaries.

Guided Learners

Recognize progress, ask questions, and explore possible next steps—with support, agency, and room to grow.

Learning Institutions

Learn from patterns in teaching and learning to improve support—without reducing people to labels or rankings.

Learning moves forward. Together.

2 · The Nine Gates — channel admission

The channel is post-class narrative debrief → per-learner sightings. Gates 1–8 pass in minutes. Gate 9 asks where extraction runs.

Gate 9 — Processing Locus: where does extraction run?

Manual sighting entry — model off

Switch the model off and record what you observed, by hand. Pick the learner and type the observation in your own words. It flows through the same verification, sign-off, and warrant trail as the automated path, but under a teacher-authored posture: the text is yours, not a quote a model pulled from the debrief. No model runs.

3 · Sightings → claims → the Twin

Evidence and interpretation stay separate. A sighting quotes the debrief — and code, not the extractor, verifies the quote is verbatim before it counts. A claim interprets sightings, waits for teacher sign-off before it enters the Twin, and can be contested, never deleted. Confidence decays at read time; drag the slider to see it.

Advisory only (DecisionJudge, D-016): a System-One-style harness over the same local Glimmer. It never admits, signs, or blocks. Slow on this hardware — about 30 s per claim.

4 · The warrant trail

Every consequential act leaves a crumb (Warrants v0 shape, ens.* namespace). Walk it: each claim traces to its sighting, its extraction, and the gate decision that allowed it.
