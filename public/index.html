3 · Sightings → claims → the Twin

Evidence and interpretation stay separate. A sighting quotes the debrief — and code, not the extractor, verifies the quote is verbatim before it counts. A claim interprets sightings, waits for teacher sign-off before it enters the Twin, and can be contested, never deleted. Confidence decays at read time; drag the slider to see it.

4 · The warrant trail

Every consequential act leaves a crumb (Warrants v0 shape, ens.* namespace). Walk it: each claim traces to its sighting, its extraction, and the gate decision that allowed it.
